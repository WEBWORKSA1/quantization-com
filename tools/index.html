Tools that show their working

Five calculators and interactive labs, one for each kind of quantization. They run entirely in your browser, store nothing on a server, and print the formula behind every result so you can check it.

LLM VRAM calculator

Weights, KV cache and overhead for any model, quant and context length — with a fit check across 20 GPUs and Macs.

GB needed · fit verdict · format comparisonOpen

Quantization playground

Quantize a signal or a weight distribution to 1–16 bits and watch the error, histogram and SQNR change live.

MSE · SQNR · levels usedOpen

ADC and bit-depth calculator

LSB size, ideal SNR, ENOB from measured SINAD, oversampling gain and audio file size in one panel.

LSB · SQNR · ENOB · MBOpen

MIDI quantize and swing lab

Play a loose drum loop, then quantize it with strength and swing and hear the difference in the browser.

Offsets in ms · audio A/BOpen

Energy levels calculator

Hydrogen transitions with photon wavelength and colour, plus particle-in-a-box energies.

eV · nm · THzOpen

Which tool do I need?

  • Downloading a local model and wondering if it will run? Start with the VRAM calculator, then compare candidates in the formats atlas.
  • Learning how quantization works or teaching it? The playground shows scale, zero-point, clipping and error on real data.
  • Choosing an ADC or audio format? The ADC calculator covers LSB, SQNR, ENOB, oversampling and file sizes.
  • Producing music? Hear strength and swing in the quantize lab.
  • Studying physics or chemistry? Work transitions in the energy levels calculator.
