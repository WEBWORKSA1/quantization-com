Quantization resources worth your time

Primary sources first: the papers that introduced each method, the libraries that implement them, and courses that teach the maths properly.

Foundational papers

  • arxiv.org

    GPTQ: accurate post-training quantization for generative pre-trained transformers

    Second-order, layer-wise weight quantization to 3–4 bits.

  • arxiv.org

    AWQ: activation-aware weight quantization

    Protects salient channels found from activation statistics.

  • arxiv.org

    LLM.int8(): 8-bit matrix multiplication for transformers at scale

    Mixed-precision decomposition that isolates outlier features.

  • arxiv.org

    QLoRA: efficient finetuning of quantized LLMs

    4-bit NormalFloat, double quantization and paged optimizers.

  • arxiv.org

    SmoothQuant: accurate and efficient post-training quantization

    Migrates activation outliers into weights to enable W8A8.

  • arxiv.org

    The era of 1-bit LLMs: all large language models are in 1.58 bits

    Ternary-weight models trained from scratch (BitNet b1.58).

  • arxiv.org

    A survey of quantization methods for efficient neural network inference

    Broad survey of uniform, non-uniform, PTQ and QAT methods.

  • arxiv.org

    Quantizing deep convolutional networks for efficient inference: a whitepaper

    Classic practical guide to integer quantization and QAT.

  • arxiv.org

    Neural network quantization for efficient inference: a survey

    Survey covering granularity, calibration and hardware.

Libraries and runtimes

  • github.com

    llama.cpp

    C/C++ inference engine and home of the GGUF format and quantize tool.

  • github.com

    bitsandbytes

    8-bit and 4-bit quantization used across the Hugging Face stack.

  • huggingface.co

    Hugging Face Transformers quantization docs

    Overview and support matrix for every integrated method.

  • docs.vllm.ai

    vLLM quantization docs

    Which quantized formats the vLLM server supports, by hardware.

  • github.com

    LLM Compressor

    Library for producing GPTQ, AWQ, FP8 and other compressed checkpoints for vLLM.

  • github.com

    PyTorch torchao

    PyTorch-native quantization and sparsity for training and inference.

  • github.com

    NVIDIA Model Optimizer

    PTQ, QAT, FP8 and NVFP4 tooling for NVIDIA deployment stacks.

  • onnxruntime.ai

    ONNX Runtime quantization

    Static and dynamic INT8 quantization for ONNX models.

  • github.com

    Intel Neural Compressor

    Quantization and compression toolkit for Intel hardware and beyond.

  • github.com

    Apple MLX

    Array framework for Apple silicon with built-in quantized model support.

  • ollama.com

    Ollama

    Runs GGUF models locally with a simple CLI and API.

Courses and long reads

  • efficientml.ai

    MIT 6.5940 EfficientML.ai

    University course on efficient deep learning, with full lecture videos.

  • www.deeplearning.ai

    DeepLearning.AI short courses

    Free short courses, including quantization fundamentals and in-depth tracks.

  • newsletter.maartengrootendorst.com

    A visual guide to quantization

    Diagram-heavy explainer of LLM quantization concepts.

  • huggingface.co

    Hugging Face Optimum: quantization concept guide

    Affine quantization maths, calibration and granularity.

Signals, audio and physics

  • www.analog.com

    Analog Devices MT-001: the SNR = 6.02N + 1.76 dB formula

    Derivation and limits of the ideal quantization-noise formula.

  • www.dspguide.com

    The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to DSP, chapter 3

    ADC and DAC: quantization, dither and sampling.

  • www.ti.com

    TI Precision Labs: ADCs

    Free video training series on converter specs and noise.

  • phet.colorado.edu

    PhET: models of the hydrogen atom

    Interactive simulation of atomic models and spectra.

  • physics.nist.gov

    NIST CODATA fundamental constants

    Authoritative values for h, e, m_e and the Rydberg constant.
