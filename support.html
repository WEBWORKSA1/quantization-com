Keep the tools free and independent

Quantization.com is free to read and free to use. Reader support pays for hosting, new calculators, the writers and engineers who check every number, promotion that brings in new learners, and the prize pool of the Quantization Challenge.

Where support goes

Our planned allocation for every dollar of general support. Supporters receive a short quarterly note on what their support funded.

30% Operations. Hosting, domains, tooling, test hardware and benchmark compute.
30% Hiring talent. Paid writers, reviewers and engineers who build tools and fact-check guides.
20% Contests and prizes. The Quantization Challenge prize pool and community awards.
20% Promotion. Reaching students and engineers through events, partnerships and marketing.

  • Report an error through the corrections form. Accuracy is the product.

Support questions

How do I pay?

After you send the pledge form we email a secure checkout link. Cards and common online payment methods are accepted there; we never see or store card numbers.

Can I cancel monthly support?

Yes, at any time, by replying to any supporter email or using the contact form. There are no minimum terms.

Do supporters influence what you publish?

No. Support funds the work; it does not buy coverage, rankings or recommendations. See our editorial policy.

Can my company support you?

Yes. Choose the Organization level, or for larger commitments and partnerships contact the owner.
