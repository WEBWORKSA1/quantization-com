Tools that show their working

Five calculators and interactive labs, one for each kind of quantization. They run entirely in your browser, store nothing on a server, and print the formula behind every result so you can check it.

Which tool do I need?

  • Downloading a local model and wondering if it will run? Start with the VRAM calculator, then compare candidates in the formats atlas.
  • Learning how quantization works or teaching it? The playground shows scale, zero-point, clipping and error on real data.
  • Choosing an ADC or audio format? The ADC calculator covers LSB, SQNR, ENOB, oversampling and file sizes.
  • Producing music? Hear strength and swing in the quantize lab.
  • Studying physics or chemistry? Work transitions in the energy levels calculator.
