Season 1 · Fit in 8 GB

The Quantization Challenge

Take an open-weight language model, make it run in 8 GB of memory at a 4,096-token context, and keep as much of its quality as you can. Any method counts: GGUF, GPTQ, AWQ, mixed precision, pruning plus quantization, your own idea.

How entries are scored

Every submission must pass a memory gate, then competes on quality.

StageRule
Memory gatePeak memory, measured by the organizers' harness, at most 8.0 GiB with a 4,096-token context and batch size 1.
Quality score (100%)Average of task scores on the hidden test set, each divided by the same base model's BF16 score. 100 means no measurable loss.
Tie-breakersLower peak memory, then earlier submission time.
Best write-up awardJudged separately on clarity, reproducibility and insight (1–5 scale per criterion).

Allowed base models are listed in the starter kit and limited to openly licensed models whose licence permits modification and redistribution.

Planned prize structure

The pool is split 50% first place, 25% second, 15% third and 10% best write-up. Winners also get a featured case study on Quantization.com. One cash prize per team.

Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. Entry is free. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  • Eligibility: individuals at least 18 years old or the age of majority in their place of residence. Organizers, judges, sponsors' staff working on the contest and their immediate families are not eligible for prizes. Residents of countries subject to comprehensive sanctions cannot receive prizes.
  • Original work: submissions must be created during the contest period by the team. Public open-source tools are allowed and must be credited. Disclose any AI tools used.
  • Reproducibility: include a public repository under an OSI-approved licence (MIT or Apache-2.0 recommended) with a README that reproduces the result.
  • Fair play: no training, tuning or calibration on the hidden test set; no manipulation of the measuring harness. One account per person.
  • Verification: potential winners must respond within 7 days and provide proof of identity and eligibility, and any tax forms required to pay prizes. Unclaimed prizes may go to the next eligible team.
  • Ownership: you keep all rights to your work. You grant Quantization.com a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to describe, display and link to your submission and write-up.
  • Changes: the organizers may amend these rules or cancel the season if fairness or legality requires, with notice on this page. Decisions of the judges are final.
