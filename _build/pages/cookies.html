Cookie policy

Effective 27 October 2026. What we store in your browser, what third parties store, and how to change your mind.

These are strictly necessary for the features you choose and contain no personal information.

Third-party cookies

  • Google AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to serve, personalise, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you choose "Essential only", we ask Google to serve non-personalised ads, which still use cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention. See how Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • YouTube videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you press play. Once playing, YouTube may set cookies under its own policy.
  • Google Fonts deliver our typefaces; Google states that it does not set cookies for this service.

Your choices

  • Reset my cookie choice to see the notice again.
  • Manage personalised ads with Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them, although ads may become less relevant.

Questions? Use the contact form. More detail is in the privacy policy.
