An excerpt from an example plan

Illustrative example for a hypothetical team, not a client result.

Setup: Llama 3.1 70B, BF16, vLLM on 4 × 80 GB GPUs, 8K context, about 30 concurrent requests.

Recommendation: FP8 weights (W8A8) with FP8 KV cache. Weights drop from about 131 GB to about 66 GB, which fits on 2 GPUs with room for the KV cache of 30 × 8K-token sequences (about 37 GB at 8-bit).

Quality check: run your 200 most common production prompts through both versions and compare with a task-specific score and side-by-side review before switching traffic.

Expected effect: half the GPUs for the same concurrency, subject to the latency test in step 4 of the plan.

Numbers computed with the method in our VRAM calculator and KV cache guide.

Questions

Is the plan really free?

Yes. It is how we learn what teams need and how some of them come to hire help later. There is no obligation and no automatic follow-up sequence.

Do you need access to our model or data?

No. We work from public model configurations and what you tell us. Please do not send credentials, proprietary weights or customer data.

Are your recommendations tied to a vendor?

No. We recommend whatever format and runtime fits your situation. If a recommendation ever involves a partner we earn a referral fee from, the plan says so clearly.

What if the model is a fine-tune or a custom architecture?

Tell us the base model and what changed. Most fine-tunes quantize like their base; custom architectures may need a calibration step, which the plan will describe.

Is our request confidential?

Yes. Details you send are used only to prepare your plan and reply to you, as described in our privacy policy.
