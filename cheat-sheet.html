Quantization cheat sheet

Every formula and rule of thumb from the site on one page. Print it or save it as a PDF from your browser.

Core quantization

q = clamp(round(x ÷ s) + z, qmin, qmax)
x̂ = s · (q − z)

Symmetric: s = max|x| ÷ (2b−1 − 1), z = 0. Asymmetric: s = (max − min) ÷ (2b − 1), z = round(−min ÷ s).

error variance ≈ Δ² ÷ 12  ·  rms ≈ 0.29 Δ

LLM memory

weights (bytes) = parameters × bits per weight ÷ 8
KV cache = 2 × layers × KV heads × head_dim × tokens × sequences × bytes

Add about 1 GB plus 5% of weights for runtime overhead. Llama 3.1 8B: 128 KiB of FP16 KV cache per token, so 1 GiB per 8K tokens.

Choosing a format, fast

  • Near-lossless: 8-bit (Q8_0, FP8, INT8 W8A8).
  • Best balance for local use: Q4_K_M to Q6_K.
  • GPU serving at scale: FP8 on newer GPUs; AWQ or GPTQ 4-bit when memory is the limit.
  • Below about 3 bits per weight, quality drops faster; use importance-matrix (imatrix) i-quants and test carefully.

Signals and ADCs

LSB = full-scale range ÷ 2N
SQNR ≈ 6.02 N + 1.76 dB  (full-scale sine)
ENOB = (SINAD − 1.76) ÷ 6.02
oversampling gain = 10 · log10(fs ÷ 2BW) dB

Each doubling of sample rate adds ≈ 3 dB (½ bit) without noise shaping.

Dither once, at the final word-length reduction (e.g. 24 → 16-bit). TPDF dither spans ±1 LSB.

Music timing

beat (ms) = 60,000 ÷ BPM  ·  1/16 note = beat ÷ 4
new time = old + strength × (grid − old)

Swing (drum-machine convention): 50% straight, ≈ 66.7% triplet. Second note of each pair sits at swing × pair length.
